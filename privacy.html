Privacy notice

This page describes the current basic behavior of the Nestora website. It is general information, not legal advice.

Information you provide

The current site does not offer accounts, comments, or a newsletter signup form, and it does not intentionally ask you to submit personal information through a form.

Marketplace preference

If your browser permits local storage, the site may store your chosen Amazon marketplace (UAE, UK, or US) in your browser so your selection remains consistent on later visits. This preference is stored locally on your device; it is not a user account.

Hosting and technical data

The site is hosted through Cloudflare Pages. Hosting infrastructure may process technical information such as IP address, request metadata, and security logs to deliver and protect the website. Refer to Cloudflare's own privacy information for details about its processing.

External links

When you follow an outbound link, you leave Nestora and visit a third-party site. That site's privacy practices, cookies, terms, and data handling apply. Amazon may use cookies or other technologies to attribute qualifying purchases according to its own policies.

Cookies and analytics

The current version does not intentionally embed a third-party analytics dashboard or advertising pixel. Browser and hosting behavior can change as the site evolves, so this notice should be reviewed whenever new tools or services are added.

Your choices

You can clear site data or local storage through your browser settings. You can also avoid following external links. Applicable privacy rights depend on your location and the services involved.

Updates

We may update this notice as the site develops. Check this page for the latest version.
